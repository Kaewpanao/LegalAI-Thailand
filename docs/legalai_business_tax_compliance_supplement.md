## Category 11: Tax & Revenue (ภาษีและสรรพากร)

Tax compliance is mandatory for every income-earning individual and business in Thailand. The Revenue Code governs most taxes, while the Land and Building Tax Act B.E. 2562 covers property tax. This category covers all major tax filings, planning tools, and related documents.

## Category 12: Corporate Compliance & Governance (การกำกับดูแลกิจการและการปฏิบัติตามกฎหมาย)

Non-compliance carries penalties and potential personal liability for directors. This category covers the essential compliance documents every Thai company must maintain.

- Tax filing templates: PND 90/91/50/51, PP 30/01, PND 3/53 — these follow government forms but can be pre-filled with merge data
- Tax reconciliation: taxable profit worksheet, extension requests, VAT refund requests
- AGM/EGM minutes templates, annual report frameworks, statutory filings (12.3–12.4, 12.13–12.16)
- Commercial registration application (13.7)

**TIER 3 — Information-Only (12 documents at LOW feasibility):**
- All Category 13 licenses are government forms requiring in-person submission, site inspections, and agency-specific processes
- Company affidavit (12.12) — must be ordered from DBD
- LegalAI should provide: application checklists, required documents lists, fee schedules, step-by-step guides, and form-filling assistance — but not auto-generate final submissions

### Revenue Model Enhancement

With these 58 additional document types:
- **Tax filing helpers**: Monetize as premium add-on during tax season (Jan–March); ฿199/set for complete personal tax package
- **Corporate compliance bundles**: ฿1,999/year for SME compliance suite (all AGM/BOD documents, annual filing reminders, document retention schedule, meeting calendar)
- **License application guides**: ฿299 each for step-by-step guides with document checklists and agency contact info (Category 13)
- **Seasonal campaigns**: Year-end tax planning (Nov–Dec), AGM season (Feb–Apr), half-year tax (Aug–Sep)

### Merge Field Architecture Additions

Additional merge fields needed for the supplement:
- **Tax fields**: TIN (เลขประจำตัวผู้เสียภาษี 13-digit), tax branch code, VAT registration number, fiscal year-end date
- **Corporate fields**: company registration number (เลขทะเบียนนิติบุคคล 13-digit), registered capital breakdown, director/authorized signatory details, auditor name and license number, fiscal year-end month
- **Meeting fields**: quorum requirements (% shareholders, minimum directors), notice period, voting thresholds (ordinary vs special resolutions), meeting type (AGM/EGM/BOD), meeting format (physical/e-meeting/hybrid)
- **License fields**: business classification codes (TSIC), factory horsepower, number of employees, land area in square wah, operating hours, manager qualification/experience
