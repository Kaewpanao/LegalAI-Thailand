### 1.2 Stream 1: B2C Consumer

**Target Market:** Thai adults (52M) with legal problems annually (~3–5% = 1.56–2.6M).  
**Channels:** LINE OA, Google Ads, TikTok, Facebook, organic referrals.  
**Strategy:** Freemium (free legal diagnosis) → Action Pack → Case Plus → Subscription.

#### Product Tiers & Unit Economics

| Tier | Product | Price (THB) | Cost to Serve | Gross Margin | Typical Use |
|------|---------|-------------|---------------|--------------|-------------|
| **Free** | Legal Diagnosis + Triage Checklist | 0 | ฿5–10 (LLM inference) | — | Acquisition funnel entry |
| **Action Pack** | Step-by-step action plan + document templates | ฿99–299 (avg. ฿199) | ฿8–15 | **92–96%** | One-off legal problem (consumer complaint, online fraud) |
| **Case Plus** | Full case workspace + AI drafting + deadline tracker | ฿399–999 (avg. ฿699) | ฿15–30 | **95–97%** | Complex case needing document drafts (divorce, debt collection) |
| **Subscription** | Unlimited Action Packs + monthly legal checkup | ฿199–499/mo (avg. ฿299) | ฿25–50/mo | **83–92%** | Repeat users, small business owners, "peace of mind" |

#### Conversion Funnel (Mature State — Year 3+)

```
Free Diagnosis Users         100%  (top of funnel)
  └─ Action Pack conversion  12–18%  → Avg. ฿199
       └─ Case Plus upsell    20–30% of Action Pack buyers → Avg. ฿699
  └─ Direct Subscription       3–5%  → Avg. ฿299/mo × 8.5 avg. months
```

#### Unit Economics Summary

| Metric | Y0 | Y1 | Y2 | Y3 | Y4 | Y5 |
|--------|----|----|----|----|----|----|
| Average Revenue Per Paying User (ARPU) | ฿320 | ฿350 | ฿390 | ฿420 | ฿450 | ฿470 |
| Average Retention (months) | 1.8 | 2.5 | 4.0 | 5.5 | 7.0 | 8.5 |
| CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) | ฿85 | ฿65 | ฿50 | ฿42 | ฿38 | ฿35 |
| LTV:CAC Ratio | 6.8x | 13.5x | 31.2x | 55.0x | 82.9x | 114.1x |

### 1.3 Stream 2: Lawyer Marketplace

**Target Market:** ~80K–100K licensed lawyers in Thailand, ~70% solo practitioners.  
**Addressable:** ~25K–35K tech-adoptive lawyers seeking client acquisition.

#### Monetization Layers

| Layer | Product | Pricing | Unit Economics |
|-------|---------|---------|----------------|
| **Matching Fee** | Client ↔ Lawyer successful match | 15–25% of transaction (avg. 20%) | Avg. case value: ฿15,000 → fee ฿3,000 |
| **Verified Profile** | Enhanced listing with badges, case history | Free (basic) / ฿500/mo (Premium) / ฿1,500/mo (Elite) | 40% free / 35% Premium / 25% Elite |
| **Fixed-Scope Consult** | 30-min video/chat consult, fixed price | ฿500–฿3,000 (avg. ฿1,200) | LegalAI takes 20% → ฿240 per consult |
| **Lead Credits** | Pay-per-qualified-lead for lawyers | ฿200–฿500/lead | Avg. ฿350/lead, 10–30 leads/mo per lawyer |

### 7.2 Growth Model Methodology

- **B2C:** S-curve adoption model with word-of-mouth coefficient (k=0.35), starting from early adopters (2.5% of addressable) to late majority.
- **Lawyer Marketplace:** Two-sided marketplace model; supply-side (lawyers) leads demand-side. Network effects accelerate after 1,500 lawyers.
- **SME SaaS:** Bass diffusion model with imitation coefficient p=0.03, innovation coefficient q=0.38.
- **Enterprise:** Direct sales model with ramping quota capacity per sales rep (3–5 deals/year/rep).
- **Government:** Discrete event model (RFP → win → implement → recurring). 15–20% win rate on qualified bids.
